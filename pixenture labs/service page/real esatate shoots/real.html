Real estate photography is a branch of commercial photography where the subject is a property that can be residential, commercial, industrial or even vacant land. And each of these categories has sub-categories as well. For example, residential properties can be family homes, condos, apartments, co-ops, etc.

As it is a commercial type of photography, the scope is typically to showcase the property's characteristics to attract potential clients to buy, rent, or sublet. Real estate photography is not about a single image; it’s always about a SERIES of photographs. You will generally need to capture not only the interior but also the building, the surroundings, etc.
